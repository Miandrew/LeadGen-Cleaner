# Prompt — Rewrite Subscription Plans & GHL Positioning

I want to redesign the subscription section of CommercialCleaningNearMe.com.

## Context

CommercialCleaningNearMe.com is **not** a GoHighLevel agency.

It is a commercial cleaning marketplace and growth platform.

Companies come here primarily because they want:

* More commercial cleaning contracts
* Faster response to new opportunities
* Better follow-up
* More Google reviews
* Better visibility
* Better organization

GoHighLevel is simply the backend software that powers some of these services. It should almost never be mentioned in customer-facing copy.

The customer is buying outcomes, not software.

---

## Rewrite the subscription plans

Replace the existing plans with these principles.

### Pay As You Go

**$35 per qualified lead**

Perfect for companies that want to try the platform with no monthly commitment.

Features:

* Purchase qualified commercial cleaning opportunities individually
* Only pay for the leads you choose
* View lead details before purchasing
* No subscription required

---

### Growth

**$199/month**

Headline:

**Win More Commercial Cleaning Contracts**

Short description:

Designed for growing commercial cleaning companies that want consistent opportunities and automated follow-up.

Features:

* Priority access to qualified commercial cleaning opportunities
* Monthly lead allocation (do NOT promise guaranteed leads—design the UI so this can be configured by an admin)
* Instant SMS follow-up for every new lead
* Missed-call text back
* Automated review request campaigns
* CRM pipeline for every opportunity
* Higher placement within directory search results
* Email support

---

### Unlimited

**$399/month**

Headline:

**Become the Highest Visibility Cleaning Company in Your Market**

Short description:

For established companies focused on growth, efficiency, and market leadership.

Features:

Everything in Growth plus:

* Highest priority for marketplace lead distribution
* Semi-exclusive leads (maximum allocation rules configurable by admin)
* Google Business Profile management workflow
* Review monitoring and response workflow
* Advanced pipeline reporting
* Monthly strategy session scheduling
* Priority support

---

## IMPORTANT

Never say:

"Includes GoHighLevel"

Never market software.

Everything should be written as business outcomes.

---

## Architecture Changes

Update the subscription logic.

The marketplace controls lead distribution.

GoHighLevel handles:

* CRM
* Pipelines
* SMS automation
* Email automation
* Missed call text-back
* Review requests
* Workflow automation
* Contact management

The marketplace controls:

* Lead generation
* Lead qualification
* Lead allocation
* Directory ranking
* Featured listings
* Subscription eligibility
* Billing logic

Keep those responsibilities completely separate.

---

Replace "CRM" wording with language business owners understand.

Review every piece of copy on the subscription page.
